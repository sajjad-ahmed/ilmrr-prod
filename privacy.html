Privacy

This page describes the public ilmrr website. It does not cover products or systems that are not linked from here.

What this site is

The site is a static collection of pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not create accounts, and it does not run a database of visitors or inquiries.

Inquiries

The inquiry form checks your message in the browser, then opens your email application addressed to inquiries@ilmrr.com. You can also copy the text and send it yourself. The website does not transmit or store the name, organization, email address, or message.

After you send the email, your mail provider handles delivery. Keep only what you need in that message.

Cookies and analytics

ilmrr does not set analytics cookies and does not embed third-party trackers. The site does not use advertising pixels.

Hosting

GitHub Pages serves the files. GitHub may keep standard connection logs for that service. Their own privacy statement describes that processing.

Contact

Questions about this page: inquiries@ilmrr.com.
